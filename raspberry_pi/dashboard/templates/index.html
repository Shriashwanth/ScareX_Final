How to Connect from Your Phone:

Option A: Same Wi-Fi Network (Home / Field Router)

  1. Connect your phone to the same Wi-Fi router as the Raspberry Pi 5.
  2. Open Chrome or Safari on your phone and browse to: http://192.168.1.xxx:5000

Option B: Standalone Field Hotspot (No Router / Offline)

  1. Turn on Mobile Hotspot on your phone. Configure your Pi's Wi-Fi to join your phone's hotspot.
  2. Alternatively, enable Raspberry Pi Access Point mode (nmcli dev wifi hotspot ifname wlan0 ssid ScareX_Field password scarex1234).
  3. Connect your phone directly to the ScareX_Field Wi-Fi and open http://10.42.0.1:5000 in your mobile browser.
